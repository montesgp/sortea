# Tracking links: which video brought each purchase (2026-11-07)

Per-video links shared on social networks, purchases attributed to the links a buyer came through, and performance reports per video, network and campaign.
Work happens on branch feat/tracking-links. Touches personal-data cookies and stored orders: migration and privacy text.

## Specs

- S1 One link per video: "Tendremos oportunidad de registrar desde que links vienen a la tienda? Generalmente se va a compartir en IG el link de la tienda y por ahi seria interesante guardar cuantas chances (en dinero total) genero cada video." In the back office, an organization creates named tracking links, each with its campaign, its network (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, WhatsApp, otra) and a short code. The link is a site URL with that code (for example `/?src=<code>`) that can be pasted in a video. Links can be archived; codes are unique per organization and never reused.
- S2 Remember the touches until the campaign closes: decision (L2) "Sí, hasta el cierre de la campaña". Entering the site through a link records, in a first-party cookie with no personal data, every link the visitor came through. Each touch is remembered until its campaign closes. No third-party trackers.
- S3 Count only purchases, credited to every link: "Tenemos forma de imputar en ambos? Es decir si entro del video A se imputan 1 acceso al video y la campania y las chances por persona y totales que consiguio el video." / "Cada vez que se entra desde un video y se compra debemos registrarlo ... Contabilizamos si es posible solo cuando compran." A purchase records all the links its buyer came through (still valid) and marks the last one. Only paid purchases count in the reports; refunded or charged-back ones stop counting.
- S4 Reports: "queremos saber cuanto rindio un video en cantidad de veces que genero al menos una compra y tambien saber cuantas chances en total genera cada video ... pueden ser pocos usuarios pero pueden ser muchas chances y mucho o poco dinero ... comparar rendimiento entre videos, por campanias y demas." Per link: paid purchases, distinct buyers, chances, gross amount and net after Mercado Pago fees, both "participó" (every link the buyer passed through) and "último" (the last link, whose totals add up to the campaign's). Group by network and by campaign, and compare campaigns. Purchases without a link appear as "Sin link", with the network guessed from the referrer when the browser sends one.
- S5 Honest limits: "Cuando me refiero al origen son redes sociales, el usuario viene desde ahi, no se si tenemos forma de determinar si vino de un video X. Si es muy dificil planteamelo para tomar decisiones". Only a link of its own identifies a video; the guide in the back office says so. The privacy policy's cookies section describes the new cookie.
